Outdoor yard: three layouts

Top-down, about 120 × 90 m each, north up. Night by default (night vision and IR matter), with the safe room in the truck or van you arrive in.

You start Enemy posts Buildings (enterable) Cover (crates, sandbags, walls) Vehicles / containers Long sightline (AWM) Wall / fence

A · Walled compound

Classic raid: breach the gate, clear the yard, then the buildings
  • Flow: gate breach → yard fight → barracks / garage → 2-floor main building.
  • Tower overwatch you must deal with first (shoot it from the van with the AWM, or sneak in).
  • Tight and fast. Closest to the current CQC feel.

B · Farm at night

Long approach over open fields, then close quarters in the farm buildings
  • Flow: sneak or snipe across the field (hay bales for cover) → stone walls → farmhouse, barn loft, silo.
  • The silo and barn loft are enemy sniper spots, with real use for night vision and IR.
  • The most open map, so the best use of the AWM, tracers and the longer flashlight.

C · Container yard

Shipping-container maze around a warehouse: mid-range, many angles
  • Flow: gate → container lanes (stack-ups, corners, climb onto containers) → office → warehouse with catwalks.
  • Crane cab is the enemy's high spot; containers give lots of half cover.
  • Fits the current gunplay best: mid-range, lots of corners, and tracers bouncing off steel.
