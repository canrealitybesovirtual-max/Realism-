# Weapon attachment database (user brief)

Game style: modern special forces / tactical mil-sim (2010-2026). Every attachment needs a believable mounting requirement, weapon compatibility and a visual model. No universal compatibility. Cosmetic parts never change performance.

## Slots per gun
- Glock 17: Optic, Muzzle, Tactical, Magazine, Sights
- AKM: Optic, Muzzle, Handguard, Underbarrel, Tactical, Magazine, Stock, Sling
- AWM: Optic, Muzzle, Bipod, Stock, Sling
- 1911: Optic, Muzzle, Tactical, Magazine, Sights, Grip Panels

## Glock 17
Attachments: red dot, weapon light, laser, suppressor, suppressor-height sights, extended magazine, optic cover.
Hardware: optic mounting plate (non-optic-ready slide), accessory rail adapter (if no rail), threaded barrel.
Rules: red dot needs optic-ready slide or plate; light and laser need a rail or adapter; suppressor needs a threaded barrel; extended magazine needs a compatible magazine interface.

## AKM
Attachments: red dot, holographic, 2x optic, suppressor, compensator, flash hider, weapon light, laser, vertical grip, angled grip, hand stop, extended magazine, adjustable stock, sling, rail covers.
Hardware: modern railed handguard, optic mounting system, accessory rail sections, stock adapter, muzzle interface.
Rules: optics need the optic mount; light and laser need a rail; grips and hand stop need an underbarrel interface; muzzle devices need a muzzle interface; stock needs a stock interface; sling needs sling points; rail covers need rails.
Progression: Standard AKM (iron sights, standard handguard, stock and magazine) -> Modernized AKM (railed handguard, optic mount, rails, modern stock) unlocks optics, light, laser and the rest.

## AWM
Attachments: variable scope, scope killflash, scope sunshade, bipod, cheek rest, suppressor, sling, scope cover.
Hardware: scope mounting system, bipod interface, adjustable stock (if required), muzzle interface, sling points.
Rules: killflash, sunshade and cover need a compatible scope objective; bipod needs its interface; cheek rest needs a compatible stock.

## 1911
Attachments: red dot, weapon light, laser, suppressor, suppressor-height sights, extended magazine, custom grip panels, optic cover.
Hardware: optic mounting plate (if needed), rail-equipped frame (light / laser), threaded barrel.
Rules: as the Glock; grip panels need a compatible frame; optic cover needs a compatible optic housing.

## Universal categories
- Mounting hardware: Picatinny rail, optic plate, optic rail system, rail adapter, railed handguard, rail sections, stock adapter, scope mount, bipod interface
- Optics: red dot, holographic, 2x, variable scope
- Tactical: weapon light, laser
- Muzzle: suppressor, flash hider, compensator
- Underbarrel: vertical grip, angled grip, hand stop
- Magazine: standard, extended
- Stock: adjustable stock, cheek rest, custom grip panels
- Accessories: bipod, sling, rail covers, scope killflash, sunshade, scope cover, optic cover

## Compatibility rules
1. Attachments only work with compatible weapons.
2. Mounting hardware must match the weapon configuration.
3. Optics need compatible mounts.
4. Lights and lasers need accessory interfaces.
5. Suppressors need compatible muzzles.
6. Grips need underbarrel interfaces.
7. Stocks must match the stock interface.
8. Magazines must match the weapon.
9. Scopes need compatible mounts.
10. Cosmetic parts never change performance.
