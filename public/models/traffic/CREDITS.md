# Highway traffic cars (`cars.json`)

All CC-BY-4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Packed by `scripts/pack-cars.mjs`: only the car's own parts kept, meshes simplified, textures shrunk and re-encoded, geometry meshopt-compressed; the Celica's wheels are cut out of its one-piece body when it loads.

- BMW E30, Renault 12, Mercedes W123 (`bmw-e30`, `renault-12`, `mercedes-w123` in `cars.json`): based on "Car Pack 1" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/car-pack-1-6d5fe4b9b8744afe9eb4616c4444886f) by buh (https://sketchfab.com/buh-late), licensed under CC-BY-4.0.
- `traffic-1` … `traffic-5`: based on "Traffic Car Pack" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/traffic-car-pack-916a8b149ae24d8b8c9cf12d4a1c64d4) by JUSTGAME (https://sketchfab.com/ayapenid), licensed under CC-BY-4.0.
- `toyota-celica`: based on "Toyota Celica Gen7 scale1-70 for 3D-printing" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/toyota-celica-gen7-scale1-70-for-3d-printing-21f0d979c3c543e79110bee451aa7014) by fishermans garage (https://sketchfab.com/fishermans), licensed under CC-BY-4.0. It has no paint of its own: it is drawn in five body colours.
